## Design Context

### Users

Prompt authors working locally, mainly on desktop, tuning structured prompts and testing them against OpenAI-compatible models.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, sharp, practical.

### Aesthetic Direction

Codex app inspired: compact panels, thin borders, neutral surfaces, restrained contrast, icon-first controls, no marketing copy, no instructional filler.

### Design Principles

- Keep the first screen as the tool, not a landing page.
- Prefer short labels, icons, and hover titles over explanatory text.
- Make editing, testing, and comparing visible without decorative layout.
- Use dense but calm spacing, stable dimensions, and subtle state changes.
- Preserve desktop ergonomics while keeping side panels usable on narrow screens.
