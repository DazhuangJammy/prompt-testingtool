## Design Context

### Users

Prompt teachers and consultants working locally on desktop, often sharing the screen
with a client to turn an unclear business process into a visible flow. They collect
client files, explain how prompts and Skills work, compare alternatives, and keep
evidence of what was tested.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, trustworthy, precise.

### Aesthetic Direction

OpenAI / ChatGPT inspired: compact panels, thin borders, neutral surfaces,
restrained contrast, and tool-first controls. The interface should feel like a calm
workbench during a client meeting, not a marketing page or a decorative whiteboard.

### Design Principles

- Keep the first screen as the tool, not a landing page.
- Make the path from client material to process, prompt, test, and conclusion visible.
- Use familiar icons with concise labels or tooltips; do not hide essential workflow
  actions behind icon knowledge alone.
- Make editing, connecting, annotating, testing, comparing, and reviewing evidence
  discoverable without decorative layout.
- Use dense but calm spacing, stable dimensions, and subtle state changes.
- Protect meeting data with autosave, clear destructive-action safeguards, and
  recoverable history.
- Preserve desktop and shared-screen ergonomics while keeping side panels usable on
  narrow screens.
